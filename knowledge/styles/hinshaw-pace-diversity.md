# Hinshaw Pace Diversity

The quality-session flavor for athletes who respond to variety: multiple
prescribed paces in one session, each anchored to a personal benchmark, with
recovery intervals treated as first-class prescription targets.

**Coaching notes.** Highly measurable despite being practitioner-grade:
every segment has a pace/watt target, including recoveries. Verify per-segment
from machine splits; score recovery-pace compliance separately from work-pace
compliance — the recovery side is the method's signature.
