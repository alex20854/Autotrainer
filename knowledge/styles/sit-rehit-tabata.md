# SIT / Wingate / REHIT / Tabata

Maximum time-efficiency tools for the metabolic track. Efficacy is grade A;
the coach's real job here is tolerability management.

**Coaching notes.** Watts verify everything: peak watts per sprint confirm
all-out effort, and a fade across sprints is *expected* — flat sprints mean
pacing, not max effort. HR is uninformative during the sprint itself
(post-sprint spikes corroborate at best). Never gate a sprint on HR. Track
self-reported tolerability each session; this is the method most likely to
quietly disappear from the week.
