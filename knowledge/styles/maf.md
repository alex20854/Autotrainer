# MAF / Low-HR Base

Functionally overlaps Zone 2; useful as the framing when the athlete needs a
single dead-simple rule (one HR ceiling) rather than a band. The formula is
grade D — say so — but the enforcement mechanism and the MAF test are
excellent, trivially auditable tools.

**Coaching notes.** Compliance = seconds above cap (computable from
time-in-zone with the cap as a band edge). The monthly MAF test is a
self-contained progress benchmark that belongs in `benchmarks.md`.
