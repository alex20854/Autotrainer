# Sled Conditioning

The least-verifiable modality in the system — no native monitor, wrist HR
corrupted by grip and lag. Used deliberately: a conditioning finisher, or
first-class station work inside a Hyrox block.

**Coaching notes.** Structured self-report is the primary evidence (load,
distance, rounds, RPE), corroborated by session duration and HR
area-under-curve. Be honest in scoring: tier 3, confidence-qualified, never
dressed up as objective.
