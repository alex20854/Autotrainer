# Short Aerobic HIIT

Billat-style time-at-VO2max work: the 20% slice when 4x4 needs variety or the
goal favors economy and rep tolerance.

**Coaching notes.** Verify by watt/pace pattern only — the sawtooth from PM5
data (rep count, per-rep watt consistency, float watts). Wrist HR is useless
in-rep (spec §5 / §8: bouts <2 min = machine metrics only); optionally use HR
to confirm cumulative drift into the high zone across the session.
