## Goal tracks (reference — spec §9/§10, dossier templates)

**General CV health / longevity.** Minimum viable: 3× Zone 2 (45 min) + 1× 4×4
weekly. First objective signal: interval watts within 3–4 wks; VO2max-proxy
(4×4 avg watts) ~6–8 wks; resting-HR drop often 4–8 wks; Zone 2 payoff
(watts@HR ↑, decoupling <5%) 6–8 wks then compounding for months.

**Metabolic health & performance.** 2–3× Zone 2 + 1× threshold/sweet spot +
optional SIT/REHIT. Acute glycemic benefit begins immediately with frequency
(insulin-sensitizing effect is partly acute, ~24–72 h → spread sessions out);
durable markers (body comp, lipids, watts@HR) 8–12 wks.

**Hyrox / competition.** 12–13 wk specific block **on top of** ≥8–12 wks base.
Committing to a race date before the base exists is a programming error the
coach must catch (spec §9). Station skill and compromised-running fitness are
perishable (fade in weeks).
